## Introduction

The University of Messina has a vibrant international community, with the Department of Economics reporting that **forty percent of its students are from abroad** [[1]].  In 2026 the university highlighted this diversity by organising an **International Students’ Day** event, hosted by the Department of Economics, to foster dialogue among students from around the world [[1]].

The 2026 celebration welcomed participants from a wide range of countries, including **Armenia, Ethiopia, Georgia, India, Brazil, Myanmar, Mongolia, Algeria, Mozambique, Ukraine, Kyrgyzstan, Pakistan and Kazakhstan** [[1]].  This initiative reflects the university’s ongoing commitment to creating an inclusive environment and supporting cross‑cultural exchange for its growing cohort of international learners.

## At a Glance

- **International community**: Forty percent of students in the University of Messina’s Department of Economics are international [[1]].
- **Student events**: The International Students’ Day 2026, organised by the Department of Economics, welcomed participants from Armenia, Ethiopia, Georgia, India, Brazil, Myanmar, Mongolia, Algeria, Mozambique, Ukraine, Kyrgyzstan, Pakistan and Kazakhstan [[1]][[1]].
- **Application portals**: Sources differ on the exact online submission process for English‑taught courses in 2027/2028. One source states that all applications must be submitted through the UniME Application Portal [[2]], while another indicates that applications must be filed simultaneously on both the UniME Application Portal and the Universitaly portal by 22 July 2027 [[2]] (conflicting information).
- **Visa requirement**: Enrollment is confirmed only after presenting a valid D‑study visa issued for the University of Messina for the 2027/2028 academic year [[2]].
- **Fees**: A €40 registration fee (covering up to two applications) applies for the 2027/2028 academic year [[2]]; the subsequent enrollment renewal fee is €156 [[3]].
- **Contact**: The Welcome Office can be reached at **foreignstudents@unime.it** for English‑taught courses and **studiainitaliano@unime.it** for Italian‑taught courses for enrolment queries [[5]].
- **Programme information**: The university lists both Italian‑ and English‑taught programmes, with entry requirements, on its website [[5]].

## Programmes and Languages of Instruction

### Programme Language Options

The University of Messina offers a mix of Italian‑ and English‑taught degrees across its faculties. An up‑to‑date list of both language streams, together with entry requirements, is published on the university’s website [[5]].

**English‑taught programmes**
- A single‑cycle Master’s degree in Medicine and Surgery is delivered entirely in English and requires candidates to sit the IMAT entrance exam [[2]].
- Other English‑taught master’s courses have their own specific academic admission criteria, which are detailed on the university’s English‑taught Programmes page [[2]].
- Prospective undergraduates must register on the UniME Application Portal, upload the required documents and obtain a pre‑evaluation of their profile before applying [[2]].
- For all English‑taught courses, foreign qualifications are submitted through the UniME Applicant Portal [[5]].

**Italian‑taught programmes**
- Italian‑language degrees accept applications via the same UniME Application Portal, but the portal used for submission is the Italian‑focused version of UniME [[5]].
- Non‑EU students enrolling in Italian‑taught programmes are admitted under the same conditions as Italian students and are **not** required to provide an Italian language proficiency test [[5]].

**Key take‑away**
- Check the online programme listings to confirm whether a course is offered in English or Italian and to see any special entry examinations such as the IMAT for Medicine [[2]][[2]][[5]].
- Ensure you use the correct portal (English‑taught → UniME Applicant Portal; Italian‑taught → Italian UniME portal) when uploading your foreign qualifications [[5]].

For any uncertainties, the Welcome Office can be contacted at **foreignstudents@unime.it** for English‑taught courses or **studiainitaliano@unime.it** for Italian‑taught courses [[5]].

## Admission Requirements

The University of Messina publishes separate admission guidance for English‑taught and Italian‑taught programmes, so start from the page that matches your course [[2]].

**English‑taught programmes**
- Prospective students should first consult the university’s English‑taught Programmes page for the specific academic admission requirements of each master’s course [[2]].
- For bachelor’s degrees taught in English, applicants must register on the UniME Application Portal and upload the required documents to obtain a pre‑evaluation of their profile [[2]].
- Successful candidates receive an admission letter after this pre‑evaluation, which must be attached to the final application package [[4]].
- Master’s applicants are subject to a verification of curricular requirements and personal preparation by a commission appointed by the programme [[4]].
- The university’s online listings provide up‑to‑date entry requirements for both English‑ and Italian‑taught programmes [[5]].

**Italian‑taught programmes**
- Non‑EU students are admitted under the same conditions as Italian students and are **not** required to provide an Italian language proficiency test [[5]].
- Applicants submit their foreign qualifications via the Italian UniME Application Portal for Italian‑taught courses [[5]].

**Application submission**
- Sources differ on whether applications for English‑taught courses must be filed solely through the UniME Application Portal [[2]] or both the UniME and Universitaly portals by 22 July 2027 [[2]]. Prospective students should verify the current process on the official website.

**Further enquiries**
- The Welcome Office can be contacted at **foreignstudents@unime.it** for English‑taught courses and **studiainitaliano@unime.it** for Italian‑taught courses for any enrolment queries [[5]].

*Note: All information reflects the latest official sources available at the time of writing. Applicants are advised to check the university’s website for any updates before submitting their applications.*

## How to Apply

1. **Create an account on the UniME Application Portal** – all English‑taught courses for the 2027/2028 academic year must be started here [[2]].
2. **Complete the online form** and upload the required documents [[2]]. This triggers a pre‑evaluation of your profile for English‑taught bachelor’s programmes [[2]].
3. **Simultaneous submission on Universitaly** – sources differ on whether this is mandatory, but one official source states that applications for the 2027/2028 intake must also be filed on the Universitaly portal by **22 July 2027** [[2]].
4. **Pay the registration fee** of €40, which covers up to two applications for the 2027/2028 year [[2]].
5. **Await the admission letter** after the pre‑evaluation; this letter is required for the visa process and must be attached to your study‑visa application [[4]].

#### Visa steps

- **Submit the study‑visa application** exclusively through the Universitaly portal, as indicated by the university (partially verified) [[5]].
- **Present a valid D‑study visa** to confirm enrollment; the university only finalises registration after this visa is issued for the 2027/2028 academic year [[2]].
- **Contact the Welcome Office** for any queries about the visa or enrolment process – use **foreignstudents@unime.it** for English‑taught courses [[5]].

> **Note:** Some details (e.g., exact visa‑submission platform) could not be fully verified from official sources at the time of writing. Prospective students should double‑check the latest instructions on the university’s official website before proceeding.

## Fees and Scholarships

For the **2027/2028** academic year the University of Messina charges a **€40 registration fee** that covers up to two applications [[2]]. Once you are enrolled, the **renewal (enrollment) fee** is **€156** per year [[3]].

#### SAW Mobility Grant

The **Students Around the World (SAW)** programme offers a **monthly mobility grant of €500 – €900**, the exact amount depending on the applicant’s ISEE (family‑income indicator) [[6]]. In addition to the monthly stipend, beneficiaries receive **extra contributions for food, accommodation and travel**, including a **one‑off travel allowance** [[6]]. The grant is intended to support students undertaking study or internship placements at **non‑European universities** that are part of the university’s international cooperation agreements [[6]].

| SAW monthly grant | €500 – €900 | Varies with ISEE [[6]]
| SAW extra support | Food, accommodation, travel allowance (one‑off) | Partially verified [[6]]

If you are considering the SAW grant, check the latest eligibility criteria and application deadlines on the university’s official SAW page, as details may be updated after this publication.

Preparing to study at the University of Messina is an exciting step, but it also involves a lot of practical planning.
